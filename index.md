# About Me

Hi! My name is Natalie Leaist and I am an Engineering Physics student at the University of British Columbia, minoring in Honours Math. I am deeply interested in the intersection of physics, electronics and mathematics. I love solving challenging problems, and I care deeply about understanding things from a fundamental level. 

I currently have a 94% average, with very strong performance in math and physics (96% average across MATH + PHYS courses). I have previous research experience in UBC's Experimental Cosmology Lab, where I worked on the CGEM project, a telescope that will be used to help detect gravitational waves from the early universe. My work involved designing and analyzing the accelerometer system for the rotating telescope mount. Additionally, I am currently an Electronic Design Intern on the R&D team at [Blue Robotics](https://bluerobotics.com/).

I have six years of programming experience, ranging from founding and leading a girls' coding club in high school to developing software for a satellite. I have built a strong foundation in Python, C, C++, Java and MATLAB. 

I am a member of the UBC Orbit Satellite Design Team. I previously worked on firmware on the command and data handling subteam. Now, I am on the Attitude and Orbit Control Systems (AOCS) Subteam, where I am helping design and build a Helmholtz cage to be used for satellite testing, as well as on ALEASIM, our control systems and orbit simulation software. On the AOCS subteam, I am gaining experience working with modelling, simulation, embedded systems, and control systems. 

Beyond Math and Physics, I am also an avid skier and enjoyer of the outdoors. I moved to Whistler in 2021 and began competing in Freeride Skiing. Freeride skiing is a type of skiing that takes place on high alpine terrain with lots of cliffs. I competed in Freeride Skiing for 3 seasons at the North American Level. In my first season (2021-2022), I finished 5th in Canada, and in my second season (2022-2023), I finished 2nd in Canada. I also qualified for the Junior World Championships. 

When I moved to Whistler, I also took advantage of other unique opportunities. I learned to mountain bike, becoming a certified instructor in my second season of riding. I also worked for two summers as an outdoor adventure guide on Canada’s 2nd longest zipline.

If you’d like to connect or learn more about my work, feel free to [reach out](https://www.linkedin.com/in/natalie-leaist). I’d love to hear from you!
