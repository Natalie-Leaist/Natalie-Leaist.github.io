*Note that courses marked with an asterisk are in progress and will be completed December 2025.*

## 300 Level Courses

| Course Code | Course Title                                      | Description |
|-------------|---------------------------------------------------|-------------|
| MATH 307*   | Applied Linear Algebra                            | Applications of linear algebra to problems in science and engineering, with emphasis on using computer systems to solve problems. |
| MATH 317    | Calculus IV                                       | A vector calculus course covering parameterization of curves, parameterizations of surfaces, arc length, vector fields, line integrals, work integrals, surface integrals, and integral theorems (Divergence Theorem, Green's theorem, and Stokes' theorem). The course concluded with an introduction to differentials forms and generalized Stokes' theorem. |

## 200 Level Courses

| Course Code | Course Title                                      | Description |
|-------------|---------------------------------------------------|-------------|
| MATH 220    | Mathematical Proof                                | A rigorous course emphasizing logic, proof methods, and proof structure. The course also taught sets, functions, cardinality, sequences, series and limits. |
| MATH 253    | Multivariable Calculus                            | A course covering vectors in 3D, partial derivatives, directional derivatives, optimization in 3D, lagrange multipliers, and muliple integrals. |
| MATH 255*   | Ordinary Differential Equations                   | Covers first and second order differential equations, linear systems of ODEs, Laplace transforms, and Fourier transforms. |
| CPEN 221*   | Software Construction I                           | An introduction to software design in Java. Topics include object-oriented design, abstract data types, testing, and verification. |
| ELEC 204*   | Linear Circuits                                   | An introductory course on electrical circuits including RLC Circuits, Laplace transforms, phasors, op-amps, diodes, BJTs and FETs. |
| ENPH 259*   | Experimental Techniques                           | A course intended for second year Engineering Physics students. Focuses on basic experimental techniques and analysis, as well as presentation and communication of results. |
| CIVL 250    | Engineering and Sustainable Development           | A course focusing on sustainability in engineering including life-cycle thinking, environmental and social impacts of infrastructure, climate change, urbanization, and transportation. |



## 100 Level Courses

| Course Code | Course Title                                      | Description |
|-------------|---------------------------------------------------|-------------|
| PHYS 119    | Experimental Physics Lab I                        | An introductory physics lab focused on data collection, analysis methods, basic statistical tests, and scientific reasoning. Data collection, analysis and lab reports were done using python and markdown in Jupyter notebooks. |
| PHYS 157    | Introductory Physics for Engineers I              | An introductory physics course covering heat, thermodynamics, waves and oscillations. |
| PHYS 158    | Introductory Physics for Engineers II             | An introductory physics course covering DC Circuits, AC Circuits, electricity and magnetism. |
| PHYS 170    | Mechanics I                                       | An introduction to statics and mechanics covering Newton's laws, force, torque, friction, impulse, momentum, work, energy, kinematics, and equilibrium of rigid bodies. |
| MATH 100    | Differential Calculus with Applications           | An introductory calculus course covering limits, continuity, and differentiation, with applications to curve sketching, related rates, and optimization. It also introduced numerical methods and multivariable calculus, including partial derivatives and critical points. |
| MATH 101    | Integral Calculus with Applications               | An introductory calculus course covering integrals, the fundamental theorem of calculus, sequences, series, and convergence tests. Applications included volumes, separable differential equations, and probability density functions. |
| MATH 152    | Linear Systems               | An introductory linear algebra course covering 3D vectors, systems of linear equations, matrices, linear transformations, determinants, and eigenvectors. Also included computer labs teaching introductory MATLAB. |
| APSC 100    | Introduction to Engineering I                     | An introduction to engineering covering the role of engineers, the engineering disciplines, sustainability, the engineering design process, group work, prototyping, technical communication, and ethics. |
| APSC 101    | Introduction to Engineering II                    | A continuation of APSC 100 focusing the design process, sustainability, prototype testing, risk management, team functioning, engineering drawings, and technical communication. |
| APSC 160    | Introduction to Computation in Engineering Design | Introduction to C Programming and Arduino. |
| CHEM 154    | Chemistry for Engineering                         | Chemistry course covering chemical bonding, properties of matter, polymers, thermodynamics, and electrochemistry. |
| WRDS 150B   | Writing and Research in the Disciplines           | An introduction to reading and writing research papers. |
